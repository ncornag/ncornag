Plan Principles
🎯
Vert specificity — Dements is 90m/km. By W18 your long run needs to match that ratio.
🥾
Power hike training — uphill hiking is a skill. Practice it deliberately on every climb. Most race time spent hiking, not running.
⬇️
Eccentric descent training — quads will determine your race. Programmed, not implied: Friday hill days from W13 are descent sessions (hike up easy, run down committed but controlled), and slow step-downs stay in every strength session.
🫀
Aerobic duration — extend, don't intensify. The Monday Z2 grows 6→10km (~45→70min) across W12–20, always capped at 151 bpm. Target ~6–7 sub-VT1 hours/week by the late build; VT1 moves on easy duration, not easy frequency. Watch long-run decoupling: second-half HR:pace drift under ~5% = durability improving.
🪨
Technical terrain — practice on rocky, ridgy ground. Serra d'Espadà is technical, not just steep.
🏔
Back-to-back long days — Sat long + Sun shorter trail simulates accumulated fatigue better than one massive day.
⛽
Fueling practice — train your race nutrition during long runs starting W12 (40–60g carbs/h), and rehearse sodium + fluids in the August heat. Gut training is mandatory for 8+ hour efforts.
🛑
Listen to the body — building to a mountain ultra at 57, smart beats heroic. Skip a session before forcing one.
