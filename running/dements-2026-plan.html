Plan Principles
🎯
Vert specificity — Dements is 90m/km. By W18 your long run needs to match that ratio.
🥾
Power hike training — uphill hiking is a skill. Practice it deliberately on every climb. Most race time spent hiking, not running.
⬇️
Eccentric descent training — quads will determine your race. Long downhills in training are non-negotiable.
🪨
Technical terrain — practice on rocky, ridgy ground. Serra d'Espadà is technical, not just steep.
🏔
Back-to-back long days — Sat long + Sun shorter trail simulates accumulated fatigue better than one massive day.
⛽
Fueling practice — train your race nutrition during long runs starting W13. Gut training is mandatory for 8+ hour efforts.
🛑
Listen to the body — at 57 with injury history, smart beats heroic. Skip a session before forcing one.

Three sessions down, half the week done — and the quality is the best we’ve seen so far. Mon (149 bpm) and Wed (147 bpm) are both clean Z2 — avg HR is finally sitting below the 151 VT1 ceiling, not kissing it. Max HR on Wednesday was 157, well inside Z3 territory only briefly; the overall picture is 100% easy, 0% tempo. That is the aerobic base signal we want.

Gym A on Tuesday is locked in: Bulgarian split squat (bodyweight, 3×8/leg — front-leg dominant), single-leg calf raise with the 3-second eccentric on the step (3×12/leg — the Achilles investment that compounds over the whole plan), glute bridge (3×15, 2-second squeeze at top), dead bug (3×10/side, lumbar flat throughout), and lateral plank (3×20s/side). Keep the form-over-load rule strict: stop one rep before form breaks.

Remaining schedule: Gym B on Thu (same circuit, aim for identical form — same weights and reps as Gym A), 5 km Z2 on Fri, and 6 km Long Z2 on Sat. Saturday is the key session of the week — your first real long-run effort. Hold 147–150 bpm throughout; if the heat pushes HR above 151 early, walk-run from the start rather than trying to slow the pace. No adjustment to Week 4 needed — the data says execute cleanly and we build from there.
