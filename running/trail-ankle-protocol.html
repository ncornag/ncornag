Trail-Ready
Ankles

Five waypoints for lateral stability, proprioception, and the strength to hold your footing on uneven ground.

Strength 2–3×/week, on non-hard-running days
Balance Daily — low-fatigue, do it while brushing your teeth
Trail rule Build load gradually. Sharp pain → stop and see a physio.
01
Waypoint 01 · Gastrocnemius · Soleus · Achilles

Calf Raises — slow single-leg + bent-knee

Watch on YouTube
3 × 15 / leg straight-leg + bent-knee
  • Full range — the heel drops below the step at the bottom, not just to floor level.
  • Lower slowly (3–4s). Keep every rep pain-free.
  • Add a bent-knee version to hit the soleus, your workhorse on long climbs and descents.

Progression: once bodyweight is easy, add load — a loaded backpack works well.

02
Waypoint 02 · Peroneals · Stabilizers

Resistance Band — four directions

Watch on YouTube
2 × 15 / direction plantar · dorsi · inv · ever
  • Work all four: point, pull-up, turn-in, and turn-out.
  • Eversion is the priority — it builds the peroneals that protect against a rolled ankle.
  • Slow and controlled. Don't let the band snap your foot back; move only the ankle.
03
Waypoint 03 · Proprioception

Single-Leg Balance

Watch on YouTube
30–60s / leg daily
  • Barefoot is best. Root the foot into the ground — big toe, little toe, heel.
  • It's fine to wobble; that's the reflexive stabilization training itself.

Progression: eyes closed → then stand on a cushion or folded towel to mimic uneven trail.

04
Waypoint 04 · Posterior chain · Balance under load

Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift

Watch on YouTube
3 × 8 / leg bodyweight
  • Hips back, not down — it's a hinge, not a squat.
  • Keep the pelvis square; don't let the free hip rotate open.
  • Reach the back leg straight behind you as a counterbalance, back flat.
05
Waypoint 05 · Reactive lateral control

Lateral Hops — stick the landing

Watch on YouTube
3 × 10–15 / leg soft landings
  • Small side-to-side hops — control beats distance (a metre is plenty).
  • Land soft with a slightly bent knee, and hold the landing a second before the next.
  • This trains the lateral control that flat-road running never develops.

ROUTE // 5 waypoints · strength 2–3×/wk · balance daily
Progress load only when form holds. New or significant ankle history → check with a physio first.
