⚡ Warm-up · 5 min
Easy bike or jog 5 min → 10 leg swings each direction per leg → 10 bodyweight squats. The deload has moved to week 11 — your vacation — so this is a full training week. Back to the full 2× / week strength + vert work. Return to week 9's loads, don't chase a new PR: you're coming off a light travel week, so the job is to re-establish strength cleanly, not to test it. Saturday's long run is a moderate 14 km / 450 m, so the slow eccentric descents stay the priority — they're what will carry your quads and Achilles through the bigger climbing weeks ahead. Week 11 is a complete break from the gym; you'll pick this straight back up in week 12.
Exercises · Strength + Vert
1
Bulgarian
Split Squat
3 × 8
/ leg
Approximate reference (dumbbell single-leg split squat) — this dataset has no Bulgarian split squat; the pattern is close, the setup differs
Target: quads
Position
  • Back foot on the bench, laces down
  • Front foot forward — knee doesn't pass the toes
Execution
  • Lower until the thigh is ≈ parallel to the floor
  • Torso slightly leaned forward
  • Push through the whole foot, mainly the heel
  • Week 10: 12 kg — back to full load after the deload; re-establish it cleanly, don't chase a new PR the first week back
Watch: knee caving inward · weight on the back leg · front foot too close. The load shouldn't break your technique — lower the weight before you lose control
2
Single-Leg
Calf Raise
3 × 8
/ leg
Target: calves
Position
  • Ball of the foot on the edge of a step, heel in the air
  • Hold something for balance, not to support your weight
Execution
  • Rise all the way up, squeeze at the top for 1 sec
  • Lower in 3 seconds — slow eccentric
  • Heel below the step for full range of motion
  • Week 10: 8 kg, 4s eccentric — vert is ramping back up; the slow lowering is what preps the Achilles for the bigger descents ahead
Watch: lowering too fast — brake more than you think you need to. Preps the Achilles for long climbs
3
Single-Leg
Glute Bridge
3 × 8
/ leg
Target: glutes
Position
  • Lying on your back, one knee bent, heel close to your glutes
  • The other leg straight or with the knee pulled to your chest
Execution
  • Week 10: 8 kg over the supporting hip — hold the load and keep the hips dead level as you resume the volume
  • Drive through the heel of the supporting leg, raise your hip
  • Hips level — don't let the free side drop
  • Squeeze the glute, hold 2 sec at the top
  • Ribs down — don't arch your lower back
  • If the weight breaks hip level, drop back to bodyweight — form rules
Watch: the free-side hip drops or rotates → shorten the range and level out before continuing. Drive from the glute, never the lower back
4
Dead
Bug
3 × 8
/ side
Target: abs
Position
  • Lying on your back, arms straight up toward the ceiling
  • Knees at 90° over your hips (tabletop)
Execution
  • Lower back pressed to the floor — the key point
  • Slowly extend the opposite arm + leg
  • Without touching the floor, return, switch sides
  • Flat back > range of motion
  • Still no added weight: progress with a 3–4 sec tempo per rep and a hard exhale, not with load
Watch: this is anti-extension control, not strength — adding weight recruits the psoas and arches your back. Back arching as you extend the leg → reduce the range
5
Side
Plank
3 × 30s
/ side
Approximate reference (incline side plank variant) — this dataset has no plain side-plank hold
Target: abs
Position
  • Side plank on your forearm
  • Body in a straight line from head to feet
  • Hips stacked, not rotated or dropped
  • Bottom leg on the floor, top leg stacked on top
Execution
  • Contract your obliques and core, keep a straight line
  • Breathe, don't hold your breath
  • Week 10: hold 30 sec steady if your hips stay level
  • Cut the time short if your hips drop — form > duration
Watch: hips dropping or rotating — cut the time before your form breaks
6
High Step-up
+ Eccentric Descent
3 × 8
/ leg
Approximate reference (dumbbell step-up) — shows the step-up pattern only; doesn't capture the box height or slow eccentric descent that define this exercise
Target: glutes
Position
  • Use the jump box — height at mid-shin / knee
  • Whole foot planted on top, not just the toes
Execution
  • Step up driving through the heel of the top leg, no push-off from the bottom leg
  • Drive the knee up — mimics the power-hike motion on a climb
  • Knee tracking over the foot — not caving inward
  • Lower in 3 seconds — the slow eccentric preps your quads for trail descents
  • Week 10: high box, controlled 4s descent — vert specificity restarts; the slow eccentric is the knees' insurance for the climbing weeks ahead
Watch: the value is in the slow descent and the higher box, not stepping up fast. Knee pain → lower the height or reduce the range
7
One-Arm
Bent-Over Row
3 × 8
/ arm
Target: upper back
Position
  • Hinge at the hips, flat back, soft knees
  • Free hand on a bench or your thigh for support
  • Kettlebell hangs straight down from the working arm
Execution
  • Pull the kettlebell to your hip, elbow close to the body
  • Squeeze the shoulder blade at the top
  • Lower under control, don't let it drop
  • Week 10: 12 kg — keeping this in for upper-back balance
Watch: twisting the torso to help the pull, or rounding the lower back — the hinge holds, only the arm moves
8
Calf Raise
Bent-Knee
3 × 8
/ leg
Approximate reference (seated calf raise) — same bent-knee/soleus mechanism, different setup (seated vs. standing on a step)
Target: calves
Position
  • Ball of the foot on the edge of a step, same setup as the straight-leg version
  • Knees bent ~30–40° throughout — this is what shifts the load to the soleus
Execution
  • Rise all the way up, squeeze at the top for 1 sec
  • Lower in 3 seconds — slow eccentric, same cue as the straight-leg version
  • Week 10: bodyweight — add 4–6 kg once the bent-knee form is locked; no rush
Watch: letting the knee straighten mid-set — that shifts the work back to the gastrocnemius and defeats the point of this variant

⚠ Warning signs · stop and reassess
  • Sharp pain (vs. muscle burn) anywhere
  • Lower-back pain after dead bugs / bridges → poor activation, don't add load
  • Knee pain in the split squat or step-up → check alignment or lower height/load
  • Achilles discomfort > 48h → reduce calf-raise volume
