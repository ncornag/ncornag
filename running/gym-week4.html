GYM
WEEK 4
Return to Trail · Foundation phase · Nico
Frequency 2× / week
Days Tue + Thu
Duration ~35 min
When After running
Rest 90s between sets
Between exercises 2 min
⚡ Warm-up · 5 min
Easy bike or jog 5 min → 10 leg swings each direction per leg → 10 bodyweight squats. Form > load — master the patterns before adding weight. Stop one rep before failure on each set.
Exercises
1
Bulgarian
Split Squat
3 × 8
/ leg
Position
  • Back foot on the bench, laces down
  • Front foot forward — knee doesn't pass the toes
Execution
  • Lower until the thigh is ≈ parallel to the floor
  • Torso slightly leaned forward
  • Push through the whole foot, mainly the heel
  • Back leg = support, 90% of the work is the front leg
  • Week 1: bodyweight
Watch: knee caving inward · weight on the back leg · front foot too close
2
Single-Leg
Calf Raise
3 × 12
/ leg
Position
  • Ball of the foot on the edge of a step, heel in the air
  • Hold something for balance, not to support your weight
Execution
  • Rise all the way up, squeeze at the top for 1 sec
  • Lower in 3 seconds — slow eccentric
  • Heel below the step for full range of motion
  • The slow descent strengthens the Achilles
Watch: lowering too fast — brake more than you think you need to
3
Glute
Bridge
3 × 12
+ 8–12 kg
Position
  • Lying on your back, knees bent, feet hip-width apart
  • Heels close to your glutes
Execution
  • Loaded: 8 kg kettlebell across your hips (move up to 12 kg once it feels easy)
  • Push through your heels, not your toes
  • Rise until shoulders / hips / knees are in one line
  • Squeeze your glutes, hold 2 sec at the top
  • Ribs down — don't arch your lower back
  • Drive up from your glutes, not your back
Watch: rest the kettlebell over your hip bone, drive up from your glutes without jerking. Hamstring cramps = bring your feet closer to your glutes
4
Dead
Bug
3 × 10
/ side
Position
  • Lying on your back, arms straight up toward the ceiling
  • Knees at 90° over your hips (tabletop)
Execution
  • Lower back pressed to the floor — the key point
  • Slowly extend the opposite arm + leg
  • Without touching the floor, return, switch sides
  • Flat back > range of motion
  • If it feels easy, do NOT add weight: slow to 3–4 sec per rep, extend the leg closer to the floor, and exhale hard to engage your core
Watch: this is an anti-extension control drill, not a strength move — adding weight recruits the psoas and arches your back. Back arching as you extend the leg → reduce the range
5
Side
Plank
3 × 20s
/ side
Position
  • Side plank on your forearm
  • Body in a straight line from head to feet
  • Hips stacked, not rotated or dropped
  • Bottom leg on the floor, top leg stacked on top
Execution
  • Brace your obliques and core, keep a straight line
  • Breathe, don't hold your breath
  • Primary work: adductors and lateral stabilizers
  • Cut the time short if your hips drop — form > duration
Watch: hips dropping or rotating — cut the time before your form breaks
✓ Expected this week
  • Muscle soreness in glutes, adductors, and calves for 2–3 days
  • Balance trouble in the split squat — completely normal
  • You'll be surprised how hard bodyweight alone is
  • Form > load. Master the patterns before adding weight
⚠ Warning signs · stop and reassess
  • Sharp pain (vs. muscle burn) anywhere
  • Lower-back pain after dead bugs / bridges → poor activation
  • Knee pain in the split squat → front foot or knee position is off
  • Achilles discomfort > 48h → reduce calf-raise volume
